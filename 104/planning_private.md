## 5. Decision log

- D1. Course is micro-economics; the guide's method is kept. Running business stays a bakery. Descriptive chapters use worked cases; calculation chapters use worked examples (Part II, section 7).
- D2. 16 chapters, numbered continuously; Ch 1-2 are the foundation chapters (slowest). 'Maths you will use' appendix at the back as settled.
- D3. Opportunity cost is taught as an idea in Ch 1 (everyday bridge) and measured in Ch 10; one cross-reference, no repetition.
- D4. Supply and elasticity of supply share Ch 7 (together about 9,000 words); demand and elasticity of demand are separate chapters because elasticity needs slow pace.
- D5. Word ruling: 'excess demand' and 'excess supply' describe market imbalance; 'surplus' is reserved for consumers' and producers' surplus.
- D6. Consumers' surplus: meaning and uses in 6.1; by indifference curves in 6.4 (advanced, deferrable); producers' surplus and the combined uses in 8.5.
- D7. Least-cost combination sits at the end of Ch 9 (needs isoquants), not in Ch 10.
- D8. Case-file figures for Ch 3-16 are pre-planned so they agree across chapters (see state file). Drafting chats may add detail but must not change fixed figures.
- D9. Practice items in the state file are written in new words with new data; none copies question wording.
- D10. Each blueprint section lists the points it must contain, so the silent coverage check can be run from the blueprint alone.
