## 1. Inventory (step 1)

Source: MGT_103.json. **10 learning outcomes**, **42 topic entries**, **50 questions** with **129 sub-questions**. **O1. Accounting as an Information System.** Define and explain the relationship among the basic accounting concepts of assets, liabilities, stockholder's equity, income and expenses.  
Topics: Business Activity; Companies; Concept and Classification of Accounting; Requirements of Accounting; Accounting Equation; Users of Accounting Information.

**O2. Accounting Cycle.** Express the effects of business transactions in terms of debits and credits to six different types of accounts and prepare a trial balance to test the equality of debits and credits in the journalizing and ledger posting process.  
Topics: Concept; Steps in Accounting Cycle; Concept of Account; Types of Account; Double Entry Accounting System; Journal; Ledger; Trial Balance.

**O3. Adjusting the Accounts.** Identify the classes and types of adjusting entries and its posting.  
Topics: Concept of Adjustments; Types of Adjustment; Reasons of Adjustments; Adjusting Entries.

**O4. Worksheet.** Prepare a work sheet, income statement, statement of owner's equity, and balance sheet using information contained in a work sheet with adjusting and closing entries.  
Topics: Concept of Worksheet; Reasons of Worksheet Preparation; Worksheet Problem Exercise of Service Company; Closing Entries; Reversing Entries.

**O5. Account of Merchandising Company.** Describe and prepare merchandising company accounts.  
Topics: Income Statement; Balance Sheet; Correction of Errors.

**O6. Special Journals.** Understand how to prepare special journals.  
Topics: Purchase Journal; Sales Journal; Cash Book; Bank Reconciliation Statement.

**O7. Accounting of Plant Assets.** Identify plant assets and calculate depreciation.  
Topics: Concepts and Nature of Plant Assets; Depreciation; Factors Affecting Depreciation; Methods of Depreciation.

**O8. Accounting for Different Types of Organizations.** Compare accounting practices among different organizations.  
Topics: Non-trading Concern; Sole Trader Ship and Partnership.

**O9. Company Accounting.** Interpret and analyze company accounts.  
Topics: Concepts; Types of Shares; Issuance of Shares.

**O10. Inventory Accounting.** Calculate cost of ending inventory, cost of goods sold, and effects on net income under the four major inventory cost methods.  
Topics: Concept of Inventory; Types of Inventory; Methods of Inventory Calculation.
