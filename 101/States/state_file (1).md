## A. BLUEPRINT ENTRIES

### Ch1 Understanding Business
Purpose: give a first-time reader the vocabulary and the picture of what business is, does and needs.
Sections: S1 Words you need first (goods, services, needs, profit, market, money, ownership; Standard x several, Minor terms) | S2 What business is: meaning, features, scope (Core) | S3 Trade, commerce, industry and their branches incl. aids to trade and e-commerce (Landmark) | S4 Functions, prerequisites for success, qualities of a businessperson (Standard/Core).
Concepts (prereq): need/want -> goods vs services -> exchange & profit -> business -> features -> industry/commerce/trade -> branches -> functions -> success conditions.
Terms: business, goods, services, profit, market, trade, commerce, industry, aids to trade, e-commerce, entrepreneur, profession, employment.
Case: 5 Jan 2026 bakery idea; production = industry; selling = trade; bank, transport, insurance = aids; no capital yet.
History (verify): early market towns and traders of Bengal.
Cross-refs: Ch2 (aims), Ch5. You will need: nothing.
Examples: familiar (tea stall) -> bakery -> online cake order (e-commerce) -> combining branches. Practice: sort activities into industry/trade/aids; explain why a hospital run for profit differs from a charity clinic; test a plan against success conditions.

### Ch2 Aims, Resources and Location
Purpose: explain why firms exist, what they use, and where they set up.
Sections: S1 Aims of business: economic, social, human, national; conflict between them (Core) | S2 Factors of production: land, labour, capital, enterprise, rewards (Landmark) | S3 Location of plant: factors, choice among sites (Core) | S4 Putting resources and place together (Standard).
Concepts (prereq Ch1 business, profit): aims -> factors -> rewards -> location factors -> site comparison.
Terms: objective, factor of production, land, labour, capital (W), entrepreneur, rent, wages, interest (W), profit, location, plant.
Case: 12 Jan 2026 targets: monthly profit Tk 60,000; rented shop 600 sq ft at Tk 45,000/month; 4 staff; planned capital Tk 12,00,000 own savings; three sites compared, Dhanmondi chosen (illustrative).
History (verify): classical economists' four factors.
Cross-refs: Ch4 (aims vs duties), Ch3. You need: profit, business, features.
Examples: weighted site table (clean scores -> messier -> new setting factory -> combined with aims). Practice: choose a site for a given firm; rank factor rewards; reconcile profit aim with a social aim.

### Ch3 The Business Environment

### Ch5 Choosing a Form of Ownership; The Sole Proprietorship

### Ch6 The Partnership

Examples: two-partner share -> three partners -> new setting -> partner exits. Practice: draw terms of a deed; compare with sole ownership; apply dissolution modes.
